# 🩺 CuroaAgent

### AI Digital Doctor powered by real-time medical research

CuroaAgent is an AI-powered medical assistant that helps users understand health concerns by researching medical information and presenting it in simple, easy-to-understand language.

Instead of relying only on a static knowledge base, CuroaAgent uses **SerpApi** to retrieve relevant medical information from the web and organizes the results into a clear response with supporting sources.

> ⚠️ CuroaAgent provides general medical information for educational purposes and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

People often search the internet when they experience symptoms such as:

- Fever
- Cough
- Headache
- Stomach pain
- Cold symptoms
- Skin problems
- Body pain
- Allergies
- Other common health concerns

However, medical information on the internet can be confusing, outdated, or difficult to understand.

CuroaAgent is designed to make this process easier.

The user describes their health concern, and CuroaAgent:

1. Understands the user's question.
2. Determines what type of research is appropriate.
3. Searches for relevant medical information using SerpApi.
4. Organizes the retrieved information.
5. Explains possible causes and important warning signs.
6. Provides relevant sources for further reading.

---

# ✨ Features

## 💬 AI Medical Chat

Users can describe their symptoms or health concerns in natural language.

Example:

> "I have had a fever for 5 days. What could be causing it?"

CuroaAgent researches the topic and provides an easy-to-understand response.
